Privacy policy

We collect the minimum needed to run and bill your account and server. This page lists everything we keep and why.

last updated 2026-11-09
What we keepWhat we do not keepCookiesWho we share it withHow long we keep itYour rightsSecurity

What we keep

  • Your email address and preferred language.
  • Your password, hashed with Argon2id. We cannot read it.
  • Login sessions: when each was created, its last activity and the browser type.
  • The IP address of requests, for attempt limits and account protection.
  • Your orders, subscriptions, invoices and their payment state.
  • The public SSH key you provide at checkout, and the server name and address.
  • Support tickets and their messages.
  • An audit record of account and server actions: who did what, and when.

What we do not keep

  • Card numbers and sensitive payment data: the payment gateway processes them and they never reach our database.
  • The contents of your server: we do not look at them.
  • What appears in the embedded console: it travels over an authenticated connection and is not recorded.

Cookies

We use one essential session cookie that page scripts cannot read. The reduced-motion preference is stored in your browser only. We use no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

  • The email provider, to deliver confirmation, password reset and notice messages.
  • The payment gateway, to complete and verify payment.
  • The competent authorities, when a binding legal request exists.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while the account exists. Financial records and invoices are kept for the period the law requires, even after an account is closed.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, its correction or deletion, and withdraw consent, under Qatar’s Personal Data Privacy Protection Law No. 13 of 2016. Send the request as a support ticket from your account.

Security

Connections to the site are encrypted. Confirmation tokens and sessions are stored hashed and used once. Sensitive actions require confirmation, and every action is recorded.
