The monthly cycle

Every server is a monthly subscription that starts on the day the first payment is confirmed. The invoice carries the plan, the region, the hostname and the amount in QAR, and appears in the billing section of the client panel the moment it is created. There is no setup fee, and the monthly price shown at order time is what you pay.

Renewal

With a saved payment method and automatic renewal on, the next month’s invoice is charged when due and you are emailed about it. Without automatic renewal the invoice is created and waits for your payment from the panel. The payment method is held by the payment provider alone; we never see or store your card number.

When a payment fails

  1. You are emailed about the failure, and the invoice stays open for payment from the panel by any method.
  2. If the invoice stays unpaid the service is suspended: the server stops and its data and address are kept.
  3. After the notice period set out in the terms of service, the server and its data are deleted permanently.

Paying the open invoice at any point before deletion restores the service as it was.

Cancellation

Delete the server from its page in the panel. Deletion is immediate and final and stops billing from the next month; copy what you need off the server first. The refund policy is on the policies page.

Account security

  • Turn on two-factor authentication in the security section of the panel: a code from your authenticator app at every login.
  • Review active sessions and end any you do not recognise.
  • Support never asks for your password, your verification code or your private key. Anyone who does is not us.
