The records you need

  • An A record for the apex (example.com) pointing at your public address.
  • An A or CNAME record for www pointing at the apex or at the same address.
  • A short TTL (300 seconds) before a planned change, and a longer one (3600) once it is stable.
bash
dig +short example.com A
dig +short www.example.com
dig +trace example.com | tail -n 5

Once the first command prints your address, the certificate in the TLS article issues successfully. Propagation takes minutes to an hour depending on the old TTL.

If your server sends email

Email from a server with no records is rejected or lands in spam. The easiest route is a transactional email service (Postmark, SES, Resend) from your application; you then add the SPF and DKIM records it asks for, and a DMARC record:

DNS
_dmarc.example.com.  TXT  "v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@example.com"

Note. The PTR record (address to name) is set on our side. If you need one for your own mail server, open a ticket with the hostname you want.
