About · Doha

About Digits Technology.

Digits Technology is a Doha company under Digits for Consulting and Trading Services. It operates Digits Cloud: virtual private servers run directly by its own team, with one client panel for servers, invoices and support.

PlansContact us
Operations · 01

How the service is run.

Infrastructure

Isolated KVM servers.

Every server is a KVM virtual machine with its own reserved resources, NVMe disk and public IPv4 address. No shared containers.

Access

Keys, not passwords.

Server login is by SSH key only, and the client account supports two-factor authentication. Support never asks for a secret of any kind.

Transparency

What is measured is published.

Each region is checked by a monitor every minute, incidents are logged publicly with their times, and the full price is shown before an account is created.
