Who provides the service

Digits Cloud is a virtual server service provided by Digits Technology, a branch registration under Digits for Consulting and Trading Services in Doha, Qatar. Registration and contact details are shown at the foot of this page.

Your account

  • You register with a valid email address and confirm it through the link we send before placing any order.
  • Your password is at least 15 characters. You are responsible for keeping it and for activity under your account.
  • You can review your open sessions and end any of them from the account security page.

What you receive

  • A virtual server with the resources written in the plan name: vCPU count, memory and NVMe storage, with the monthly transfer stated on the plan page.
  • Full root access. Login is by SSH key only; we never send server passwords.
  • One public IPv4 address per server, as listed on the plans page.
  • A workspace for power actions, invoices and support tickets.

Orders and payment

  • The price is the one shown in Qatari riyals at checkout, calculated by our system rather than by the browser page.
  • Billing is monthly and in advance. Each invoice is numbered by our system and appears in your account.
  • Payment is taken through an online payment gateway. We do not store card numbers.
  • The server is created after the gateway confirms payment, not when the order button is pressed.

Renewal

  • Automatic renewal is optional and stays off until you enable it for a subscription.
  • We send an email notice when a renewal invoice is issued.
  • If a renewal invoice is not paid we may suspend the server after notifying you, and delete it after the period stated in that notice.

Acceptable use

The service may not be used for any of the following:

  • Sending spam or fraudulent messages, or hosting phishing pages.
  • Attacking other systems or networks, or scanning them without their owners’ permission.
  • Distributing or controlling malicious software.
  • Infringing intellectual property, or any activity that breaks the laws of the State of Qatar.
  • Circumventing resource limits or the isolation of other servers.

Your data and backups

What is on your server is yours and your responsibility, including its backups. Snapshots, where available in your region, are kept on the same infrastructure and are not an off-server backup.

Availability

We publish the state of each region and the incident record on the status page. We do not commit to an availability figure that is not published there, and we announce planned maintenance in advance.

Suspension and termination

  • You can cancel a subscription at any time from your workspace; the service continues until the end of the paid period.
  • We may suspend a server that breaks acceptable use immediately to protect the network, and we tell you why.
  • Deleting a server is permanent and cannot be undone, which is why it asks for a second confirmation.

Limits of liability

To the extent the law allows, our liability for any loss arising from the service is limited to what you paid for the affected period. We are not liable for data that was not backed up or for lost profits.

Law and jurisdiction

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Qatar, and its courts have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them.

Changes to these terms

If we change these terms we publish the new version here with its date and notify account holders by email before it takes effect.
