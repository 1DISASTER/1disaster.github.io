Refund and cancellation policy

This page states when you get back what you paid, how to ask for it, and where a complaint goes if we do not resolve it.

last updated 2026-11-09

Cancelling before the server is created

If you have paid, your server has not been created yet and you ask to cancel, you are refunded in full.

After the server is created

  • The current monthly period is not refunded in part once it has started.
  • Any period paid in advance that has not started yet is refunded when you ask to cancel.
  • Cancelling a subscription stops renewal; the server keeps running until the end of the paid period.

If we cannot deliver the service

If we cannot create your server, or the service stops because of us and we cannot restore it, you are refunded for the period that was not delivered.

What is not refunded

  • A service suspended or ended for breaking acceptable use.
  • A period that has been fully used.

How to request a refund

  • Open a support ticket from your account and name the server or the invoice number.
  • An authorised person reviews the request: no refund is issued automatically.
  • The amount is returned to the same payment method you paid with.

Complaints

Start with a support ticket so the complaint has a record. If it is not resolved, you may take it to the Consumer Protection Department at the Ministry of Commerce and Industry of the State of Qatar.
